Privacy Policy

Dino Rampage · Effective November 14, 2026 (updated for in-app purchases)

Dino Rampage is a game made by Alex Gleyzer. Your privacy matters, and this game is built to collect as little as possible.

The short version

Dino Rampage does not ask for your name, email, or contacts, and does not sell your personal information. It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It has no accounts. It uses Google AdMob to show a banner ad on the Game Over screen and Google Analytics for Firebase to see how players progress through levels. These services collect some device and usage information, described below.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes levels cleared, stars, scores, and your settings (like sound and layout). Your saved progress itself is never sent to me.

In-app purchases

Dino Rampage offers optional purchases (a Skins Pack and Remove Ads). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Apple of which items you own, so it can unlock them, and remembers this on your device.

Ads (Google AdMob)

A banner ad appears only on the Game Over screen, never while you play. The ad is provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device type, an app-specific device identifier, your approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with ads. The game does not request permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn which levels are too hard or too easy, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase, such as starting, clearing, or failing a level, along with basic device and app information. No names, emails, or Apple IDs are sent.

Children's privacy

Dino Rampage does not knowingly collect personal information such as names or contact details from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
