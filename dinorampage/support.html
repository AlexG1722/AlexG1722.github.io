Contact

Email alexgleyzer12@gmail.com with your question. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug. I usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Steer your dinosaur with the on-screen controller, charge into monsters, and lunge to take them down. Clear each level's goal to unlock the next one. There are 60 levels, and superpowers like stomp and laser help in tougher fights.

Can I play full screen?

Yes. The game starts with the split layout (game on top, controller below). You can switch to full screen in the settings.

Does it work on foldable iPhones?

Yes. When half-folded, the game shows on one half and the controller and menus on the other.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Why do I see ads?

A small banner ad shows on the Game Over screen only, never during play. It helps keep the game free. You can remove it for good with the Remove Ads purchase in the Shop.

Can I continue after dying?

Yes, once per level. On the Game Over screen, tap Watch ad to continue to come back right where you fell with full hearts.

How do I change my dinosaur's look?

Open the 🛒 Shop from the main menu or the Game Over screen. The Skins Pack unlocks every skin, including Golden Rex. Tap a skin to use it.

I bought something but don't have it

Open the 🛒 Shop and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Purchases carry over to your other devices the same way.
